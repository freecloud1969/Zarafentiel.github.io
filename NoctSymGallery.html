Nocturnal Symphony Presentation
Contact

Inspired by the dark gothic films and books I consumed growing up I made a nocturnal symphony collection. Each look is made to tell a story, each picture shot to encapsulate the love, anger, and loss present in many of these tales. This collection blends gender traditions and power, playing with the dark desires of the genre. The sharply fitted clothes and limited palette of reds and blacks recalls the eternally beautiful vampire, and the often tragic world it lives in. Much like these creatures, these models live in the music of the nocturnal symphony.
