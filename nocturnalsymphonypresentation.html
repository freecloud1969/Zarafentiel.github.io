Nocturnal Symphony Presentation
Contact

Nocturnal Symphony is the culmination of my education at Drexel University. Taking both feminine and masculine Victorian gothic silhouettes, I designed ten and sewed three looks. This presentation takes a walk through my process from the customer profile, to color palettes, illustrations, and flat sketches. Each look is tailored to the models and completed with hand done couture finishes. Most of the fabric is upcycled or hand dyed, keeping in line with my priority of being environmentally conscious.
