Zarafentiel Dupont is a costume designer based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Graduating in 2025 with a degree in fashion design from Drexel University, they have worked in both film and alongside independent designers.

They have a deep passion for blending historical fashion, environmentally conscious design, and alternative subcultures for the modern eye. Their work takes from historical silhouettes and combines it with a long standing love for horror and gothic media. Such works include the collection Nocturnal Symphony and the award-winning short film Faker (2025).

Through collaboration and creativity, Zarafentiel strives to make stories that inspire others and make the world a more beautiful place.
